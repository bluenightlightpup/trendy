Trendy — Support

Trendy decodes slang, texting abbreviations and meme trends, judgment-free. It works offline and needs no account.

Decode gave a weak or wrong answer

Answers marked Lower confidence (Pattern guess, Best guess, Dictionary) mean the term is not in the lexicon yet. Try a different spelling or just the key word. Report missing or wrong terms using the contact link below.

How fresh is the trend list?

The catalog ships inside the app and is refreshed with app updates.

Does Trendy collect data?

No. See the privacy policy. The only optional network request is a single-word dictionary lookup, which you can switch off under You → Decode.

Reset the app

You → Saved trends → Clear saved trends. Deleting the app removes all of its data.
