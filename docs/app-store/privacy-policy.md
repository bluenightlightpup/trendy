# Trendy for iOS — Privacy Policy

**Effective:** October 5, 2026 · **App:** Trendy (iOS) · **Developer:** bluenightlightpup

Trendy explains slang, texting abbreviations and meme trends. It is built to work on your device without an account.

## The short version

- **We collect no data.** Trendy has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Your saved trends and preferences are stored only on your iPhone.
- **One optional network request:** when *Dictionary lookups* is on and you decode a **single word**, the app asks the public Free Dictionary API for extra meanings of that word. Only the word is sent.

## What is stored on your device

Trendy stores these items in iOS's standard app storage (UserDefaults) on your device:

- trends you save (heart),
- the worlds you switch on or off, your digest setting, *New here* mode and the *Dictionary lookups* setting.

This data is never sent to us or anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app. Like other app data, it may be included in your own device backups (iCloud or computer) depending on your iOS settings; we cannot access those backups.

Decode conversations are kept in memory only and are cleared when the app closes.

## Optional dictionary lookup

The trend catalog and slang lexicon are bundled inside the app and work offline. For single-word queries (for example "rizz", not "nah I'd win"), Trendy can also fetch dictionary definitions:

- **What is sent:** the word you searched, in an HTTPS request to `https://api.dictionaryapi.dev`. No account, device identifier, advertising identifier, location or cookies are sent. As with any internet request, the service can see your IP address.
- **Who runs it:** the [Free Dictionary API](https://dictionaryapi.dev/) is an independent public service; we do not operate it, receive its logs or share anything else with it. Its own practices apply to requests it receives.
- **How to turn it off:** open **You → Decode → Dictionary lookups** and switch it off. Decode then works fully offline.

Multi-word phrases are never sent. If the lookup fails or you are offline, Trendy quietly shows its offline answer.

## Children

Trendy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app explains some adult slang in plain, non-graphic language so parents and caregivers can understand it; swear words are masked.
