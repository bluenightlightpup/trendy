Trendy — Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 · Trendy for iOS · Developer: bluenightlightpup

The short version

  • We collect no data. No accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • Everything stays on your device. Saved trends and preferences are stored only on your iPhone.
  • One optional network request: with Dictionary lookups on, decoding a single word asks the public Free Dictionary API for extra meanings. Only the word is sent.

What is stored on your device

Trendy keeps the trends you save, the worlds you switch on or off, your digest setting, New here mode and the Dictionary lookups setting in iOS's standard app storage (UserDefaults) on your device. This data is never sent to us or anyone else and is removed when you delete the app. Like other app data it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which we cannot access.

Decode conversations are kept in memory only and are cleared when the app closes.

Optional dictionary lookup

The trend catalog and slang lexicon are bundled inside the app and work offline. For single-word queries (for example “rizz”, not “nah I'd win”), Trendy can also fetch dictionary definitions:

  • What is sent: the word you searched, over HTTPS, to api.dictionaryapi.dev. No account, device or advertising identifier, location or cookies. As with any internet request, the service can see your IP address.
  • Who runs it: the Free Dictionary API is an independent public service. We do not operate it, receive its logs or share anything else with it.
  • Turn it off: You → Decode → Dictionary lookups. Decode then works fully offline.

Multi-word phrases are never sent. If the lookup fails or you are offline, Trendy quietly shows its offline answer.

Links

Privacy, support and credits links open in Safari. Websites you visit there have their own policies.

Children

Trendy does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It explains some adult slang in plain, non-graphic language so parents and caregivers can understand it; swear words are masked.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its effective date, and note material changes in the App Store release notes.
